Flo Brown

Flo Brown, known as the Detroit Chitterling Queen, brings seasoned soul food and catering to the Detroit community. Every plate is prepared with local flavor and care — from chitterlings and comfort classics to event catering for families and gatherings.

She’s become a trusted local food voice, known for reviews, popups, and community events around Detroit.
